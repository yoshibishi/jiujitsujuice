MAT GLOW is designed for everyday skincare, combining hydration with ingredients selected to support a smoother, healthier-looking complexion. How to use

After cleansing, apply a few drops evenly across the face and neck. Gently massage or pat until absorbed. Use before moisturizer in your morning or evening routine. If layering with Tap Out, apply Tap Out to targeted areas first. Start once daily; increase to morning and evening use if well tolerated. Finish your morning routine with broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen.

Skincare tips

For external use only. Patch test before first use, especially if you have sensitive skin. Avoid applying to broken or irritated skin, and discontinue use if irritation occurs. Avoid direct contact with eyes. Introduce new products one at a time to assess skin tolerance. During the day, use broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen.
