Mat Glow

Daily firming + hydration
$40 / 30 mL
Estimated 1–3 months with regular use · Individual usage may vary

MAT GLOW is designed for everyday skincare, combining hydration with ingredients selected to support a smoother, healthier-looking complexion. The formula features 1% GHK-Cu copper peptide, hyaluronic acid, Matrixyl 3000, and Argireline. These ingredients are included to help maintain moisture and improve the appearance of skin texture and fine lines. Lightweight and easy to incorporate into a morning or evening routine.

Key ingredients

  • 1% GHK-CuA copper peptide used in skincare to support the appearance of smoother, healthier-looking skin.
  • Hyaluronic AcidA humectant that attracts and retains moisture, helping skin appear hydrated and plump.
  • Matrixyl 3000A peptide complex used in skincare to help improve the appearance of fine lines and skin texture.
  • ArgirelineA cosmetic peptide used to help reduce the appearance of expression lines.

Ingredient highlights describe the formulation; they do not establish clinical outcomes.

How to use

After cleansing, apply a few drops evenly across the face and neck. Gently massage or pat until absorbed. Use before moisturizer in your morning or evening routine. If layering with Tap Out, apply Tap Out to targeted areas first. Start once daily; increase to morning and evening use if well tolerated. Finish your morning routine with broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen.

Check the finished product label for the most current ingredient declaration.

Skincare compatibility

Mat Glow contains concentrated copper peptides (GHK-Cu). As a precaution, use low-pH vitamin C serums (L-ascorbic acid) and strong exfoliating acids (AHAs/BHAs) in a separate skincare routine. Retinoids may also be used in a separate routine initially, especially if your skin is sensitive or prone to irritation. For example, use vitamin C in the morning and your copper-peptide serum in the evening. Introduce new active ingredients gradually and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

Skincare tips

For external use only. Patch test before first use, especially if you have sensitive skin. Avoid applying to broken or irritated skin, and discontinue use if irritation occurs. Avoid direct contact with eyes. Introduce new products one at a time to assess skin tolerance. During the day, use broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen.
