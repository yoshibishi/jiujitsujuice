The fundamentals come first.

Clean Slate starts your skincare routine with a simple cleansing step. Its formula includes glycerin and panthenol.

Highlights

  • Fragrance-free
  • Sulfate-free
  • Glycerin
  • Panthenol

How to use

Apply to wet skin, gently massage over the face, and rinse thoroughly. Follow with your preferred serum, if using one, and moisturizer.

Full ingredients (INCI)

Water (Aqua), Decyl Glucoside, Cocamidopropyl Betaine, Glycerin, Sodium Chloride, Panthenol, Xanthan Gum, Phenoxyethanol, Tetrasodium Glutamate Diacetate.

For external use only. Avoid direct eye contact. Discontinue use if irritation occurs. Patch test before first use.
