About our peptides

We use ≥99% purity peptide powders instead of pre-diluted solutions. Our GHK-Cu percentages are true concentrations of pure copper peptide: Mat Glow contains 1% GHK-Cu and After Roll contains 2% GHK-Cu, measured by weight of the finished serum. Our SNAP-8 concentration is equivalent to a 10% solution, and our Syn-Ake to a 4% solution, measured against a standard 1% active booster. Mat Glow’s Matrixyl 3000 (3%) and Argireline (2%) are listed by solution percentage, the industry standard for those ingredients.
