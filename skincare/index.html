Tap Out

Isolated wrinkle-line treatment
$30 / 30 mL

TAP OUT is a targeted facial serum designed to help soften the appearance of expression lines, particularly on the forehead, between the eyebrows, and around the outer eye area. Featuring Syn-Ake, a synthetic peptide used in cosmetic anti-wrinkle formulations, alongside aloe vera leaf juice and skin-conditioning ingredients, this lightweight treatment focuses on a smoother-looking complexion. Apply to specific areas of concern as part of your daily skincare routine.

  • Syn-Ake
  • Aloe Vera Leaf Juice
  • Dipeptide
  • Sodium Glutamate Diacetate
How to use & ingredients

How to use

Apply a small amount to clean, dry skin, focusing on areas where expression lines are most noticeable, such as the forehead, between the eyebrows, and around the outer eye area. Gently pat until absorbed. Follow with your regular moisturizer or other facial serums. Avoid direct contact with the eyes. Start with once-daily application and adjust based on skin tolerance.

Full ingredients

Water (Aqua), Hydroxyethylcellulose, Phenoxyethanol, Dipeptide Diaminobutyroyl Benzylamide Diacetate, Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice, Tetrasodium Glutamate Diacetate.

Mat Glow

Daily firming + hydration
$40 / 30 mL

MAT GLOW is designed for everyday skincare, combining hydration with ingredients selected to support a smoother, healthier-looking complexion. The formula features 1% GHK-Cu copper peptide, hyaluronic acid, Matrixyl 3000, and Argireline. These ingredients are included to help maintain moisture and improve the appearance of skin texture and fine lines. Lightweight and easy to incorporate into a morning or evening routine.

  • 1% GHK-Cu
  • Hyaluronic Acid
  • Matrixyl 3000
  • Argireline
How to use & ingredients

How to use

After cleansing, apply a few drops evenly across the face and neck. Gently massage or pat until absorbed. Use before moisturizer in your morning or evening routine. If layering with Tap Out, apply Tap Out to targeted areas first. Start once daily; increase to morning and evening use if well tolerated. Finish your morning routine with broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen.

Full ingredients

Water (Aqua), Glycerin, Butylene Glycol, Carbomer, Polysorbate-20, Copper Tripeptide-1, Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1, Palmitoyl Tetrapeptide-7, Acetyl Hexapeptide-8, Acetic Acid, Phenoxyethanol, Potassium Sorbate, Sodium Hyaluronate, Benzoic Acid, Dehydroacetic Acid, Sodium Hydroxide.

After Roll

Post-BJJ recovery shield
$50 / 30 mL

AFTER ROLL is formulated for skin exposed to regular training, including sweat, friction, and frequent showering. Featuring 2% GHK-Cu copper peptide, SNAP-8, squalane, and skin-conditioning emollients, this serum focuses on replenishing moisture and supporting the skin barrier. Its moisturizing formula helps skin feel softer, smoother, and more comfortable after training. Ideal for post-BJJ skincare or evening use when your skin needs extra hydration.

  • 2% GHK-Cu
  • SNAP-8
  • Squalane
  • Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride
How to use & ingredients

How to use

After training, cleanse your face to remove sweat and surface impurities, then gently pat dry. Apply a few drops to the face and neck, concentrating on areas that feel dry or uncomfortable. Gently massage until absorbed. Follow with moisturizer if additional hydration is needed. After Roll can also be used in an evening skincare routine, including on rest days.

Full ingredients

Water (Aqua), Propanediol, Squalane, Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Copper Tripeptide-1, C13-15 Alkane, Tocopheryl Acetate, Hydroxyethylcellulose, Sodium Acrylate/Sodium Acryloyldimethyl Taurate Copolymer, C15-19 Alkane, Polyglyceryl-6 Laurate, Polyglycerin-6, Ethylhexylglycerin, Caprylyl Glycol, Phenoxyethanol, Acetyl Octapeptide-3, Sodium Gluconate, Sorbic Acid.

Skincare Tips

For external use only. Patch test before first use, especially if you have sensitive skin. Avoid applying to broken or irritated skin, and discontinue use if irritation occurs. Avoid direct contact with eyes. Introduce new products one at a time to assess skin tolerance. During the day, use broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen.

About Our Peptides

We formulate our serums using ≥99% purity GHK-Cu, SNAP-8, and Syn-Ake peptide powders, rather than pre-diluted peptide solutions. Each batch of peptide powder comes with a supplier-provided Certificate of Analysis (COA) documenting its reported purity and quality specifications.

For example, our GHK-Cu concentrations reflect the amount of pure copper peptide in the finished serum, measured by weight. Mat Glow contains 1% GHK-Cu, while After Roll contains 2% GHK-Cu.

We carefully select our ingredients to create targeted skincare formulas designed for hydration, skin conditioning, and improving the appearance of fine lines.
