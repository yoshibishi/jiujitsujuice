Skincare Tips

For external use only. Patch test before first use, especially if you have sensitive skin. Avoid applying to broken or irritated skin, and discontinue use if irritation occurs. Avoid direct contact with eyes. Introduce new products one at a time to assess skin tolerance. During the day, use broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher sunscreen.

About Our Peptides

We formulate our serums using ≥99% purity GHK-Cu, SNAP-8, and Syn-Ake peptide powders, rather than pre-diluted peptide solutions. Each batch of peptide powder comes with a supplier-provided Certificate of Analysis (COA) documenting its reported purity and quality specifications.

For example, our GHK-Cu concentrations reflect the amount of pure copper peptide in the finished serum, measured by weight. Mat Glow contains 1% GHK-Cu, while After Roll contains 2% GHK-Cu.

We carefully select our ingredients to create targeted skincare formulas designed for hydration, skin conditioning, and improving the appearance of fine lines.

Skincare information is educational and not medical advice. Ingredient compatibility, preservation, stability, labeling and safety of a finished formula require independent verification. Ask your coach for current details and pricing.
